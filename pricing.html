Quick answer

What does a fractional CTO cost?

A fractional CTO from Fraction costs $6,000–$18,000/month across three retainer tiers — Advisory ($6k, ~1 day/week), Embedded ($12k, ~2–3 days/week) and Command ($18k, ~3–4 days/week) — billed month-to-month with no equity and no long contract. Prefer to start small? The fixed-fee Tech Teardown is $7,500 for a two-week review, creditable toward your first month. See how this compares to a full-time CTO hire, or book a 20-minute fit call to size the right tier.

Questions, answered

How much does a fractional CTO cost?

Fraction's retainers run $6,000–$18,000/month across three tiers — Advisory ($6k), Embedded ($12k) and Command ($18k) — plus a one-off $7,500 Tech Teardown. For a deeper breakdown of what drives the number, read what a fractional CTO costs in 2026.

Do you require equity or a long-term contract?

No. Every retainer is month-to-month with 14 days' notice to cancel and no equity required. A reduced-cash-plus-small-equity structure is available for the right early company — just ask. More on the equity question here.

Why is there no hourly rate?

Because the moment you're watching a clock, you stop calling with the decisions that actually matter. A retainer means you get judgment whenever you need it — that's where the value is. See how the engagement works.

What if I'm not ready to commit to a monthly retainer?

Start with a fixed-fee Tech Teardown: $7,500 for a two-week review of your code, architecture, team and spend, ending in a written verdict and prioritized fix list. Roll the fee into your first month if you go monthly within 30 days.

How do I know which tier I need?

Advisory suits pre-seed/seed teams with 1–4 developers; Embedded suits seed–Series A teams of 3–15 actively hiring; Command suits funded companies between CTOs. Not sure if you even need one yet? Compare fractional vs full-time, then book a 20-minute fit call and we'll size it together.
